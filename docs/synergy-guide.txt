PANDEMONIA TCG — ABILITY SYNERGY GUIDE
=======================================
Ratings: S > A > B > C
S = game-winning combo, win condition on its own
A = meaningfully stronger than the sum of parts, often a deck's core plan
B = real upside, requires setup or specific conditions
C = situational bonus, niche but valid

Synergies are keyword×keyword pairs (or keyword×environment/status). Three-way
combos are noted where all three must be present to reach the listed tier.


════════════════════════════════
S-TIER — Win conditions
════════════════════════════════

▸ Overshot + Double Strike
  Double Strike attacks twice; Overshot routes both hits directly to the enemy
  leader past all blockers. A 4-attack body deals 8 face damage per turn with
  zero counterplay except killing the unit outright. Top offensive combo in the
  game — the Combo deck's endgame.

▸ Lethal + Sniper
  Lethal kills on any damage. Sniper can target any unit on the board from the
  Heights lane. Together: one unit eliminates any threat on the enemy's entire
  board every combat. No board position is safe, no back-row is protected.

▸ Lethal + Double Strike
  Two guaranteed kills per combat. Even a 1-attack Lethal+DoubleStrike body
  dismantles wide boards in two swings. Massacres Swarm strategies entirely.

▸ Polish + recurring damage environment (Cinder Field / burn lane)
  Polish gains permanent stats on every damage event. A Cinder Field deals 1
  damage/turn to all units in the lane — your Polish unit uses that tick to
  snowball for free, every turn, without the opponent being able to stop it
  short of removing the environment. Exponential scaling disguised as chip
  damage. Enabled by: Cinder Field, Molten Floor (burn), or enemy burn spells
  aimed at your lane.

▸ Foundation grant → Battle Ready (Combo bond)
  Pre-place a grant foundation (Overshot / Double Strike / Growth / Lethal).
  On a later turn, bond a high-attack body: it arrives Battle Ready AND carries
  the grant keyword. A 5-attack body + Overshot + Battle Ready = 5 unblockable
  face damage the very turn it enters, before the opponent can respond. The
  Combo deck's primary win condition.


════════════════════════════════
A-TIER — Core plan enablers
════════════════════════════════

▸ Airborne + Sniper
  Sniper is normally restricted to the Heights lane ("from Heights, may attack
  any lane"). Airborne makes ANY lane count as Heights for the unit. Result:
  the Sniper's reach is always live regardless of where it sits, and one removal
  answer (killing the Heights unit) no longer turns it off.

▸ Bloodlust + Double Strike
  Two attacks per combat = two chances to kill = two Bloodlust triggers.
  A Bloodlust unit that gains +1/+1 per kill snowballs to a 6/6+ inside two
  turns with Double Strike on an open board.

▸ Bloodlust + Splash Damage
  Splash Damage hits the unit's lane and both neighbouring lanes simultaneously.
  If multiple units die in that sweep, each kill fires a separate Bloodlust
  trigger. A Bloodlust+Splash unit can triple-stack in a single combat round.

▸ Bloodlust + Lethal
  Lethal kills on 1 damage. Bloodlust fires on each kill. Even into a row of
  5-HP defenders a Lethal+Bloodlust unit trades up and grows from every kill.
  The pair is an exponential kill machine that outscales any board if left alive.

▸ Overshot + Lethal
  Lethal kills on minimum damage; remaining attack overflows to the leader.
  A 6-attack Lethal+Overshot unit vs a 3-HP blocker: 1 damage kills the body,
  5 damage hits the leader. Against 1-HP tokens: 5 free face each swing.
  Best with high attack; punishes token floods hard.

▸ Growth + Immunity
  Growth accumulates permanent stat increases. Immunity prevents targeting by
  spells, abilities, and status effects. The opponent is forced to kill it with
  combat bodies — which takes multiple turns — while it grows larger each turn.

▸ Growth + Taunt
  Paradox wall: Taunt forces the opponent to keep attacking it, which buys more
  Growth turns. The more they commit to killing it, the bigger it gets. Stall
  decks weaponise this to buy an insurmountable late game.

▸ Spike + Taunt
  Taunt funnels all enemy attacks into the Spike unit. Every attacker takes
  reflected Spike damage just for engaging. High-Spike+Taunt bodies are
  extremely punishing to attack into — the opponent must sacrifice HP from their
  own units just to trade.

▸ Tough + Spike
  Tough reduces incoming damage; Spike returns damage at full value. The
  attacker triggers Spike for full damage while Tough absorbs part of the return.
  Very hard to profitably attack into — attackers take full Spike, deal
  reduced damage, and cannot kill it efficiently.

▸ Airborne + Overshot
  Overshot hits the leader past blockers. Airborne means the unit can intercept
  enemy Overshot attacks AND ignores lane positioning advantages. Airborne frees
  the Overshot unit from being trapped in a crowded lane or countered by a
  strategic ground block.

▸ Aquatic + Overshot (or Double Strike / Sniper)
  Non-aquatic enemies in the Water lane Drown to 0 attack — they become pure
  bodies, unable to retaliate. Your aquatic aggressive unit hits face, picks
  targets, or swings twice in total safety. The water lane becomes a one-way
  kill box.


════════════════════════════════
B-TIER — Real upside, needs setup
════════════════════════════════

▸ Lethal + Splash Damage
  Splash hits the unit's own lane and both neighbours. Lethal kills anything
  it touches. With a wide enemy board, one Lethal+Splash attack can kill up to
  three units simultaneously (one per lane). Hard counter to board-flooding.

▸ Lethal + Branch Shot
  Branch Shot attacks both neighbouring lanes (no retaliation). Lethal makes
  both hits lethal. Two guaranteed kills, zero return damage.

▸ Lethal + Pierce
  Pierce bypasses Shield, Taunt, Spike, and Tough to hit the deepest unit.
  Lethal kills it. Surgical removal of protected back-row supports or
  growth engines that the enemy has walled off.

▸ Lethal + Strike Through
  Strike Through damages the blocker AND the enemy leader simultaneously.
  Lethal kills the blocker; the leader also takes the full attack damage.
  Guaranteed kill + guaranteed face damage in one swing.

▸ True Shield + Spike
  First hit is absorbed completely by True Shield (unit takes 0). The attacker
  still triggers Spike and takes reflected damage. Extremely punishing to attack
  into — the attacker eats Spike for free, the True Shield unit survives unharmed.

▸ Shield + Spike
  Same principle as True Shield + Spike but stackable: each shielded hit
  triggers Spike while the unit takes 0 damage. Stack Shield charges for a
  sustained Spike wall.

▸ Zombified + Kamikaze
  Zombified revives at 1 HP on first death; Kamikaze fires its death effect on
  destruction. Result: Kamikaze fires TWICE — once on initial death (unit
  revives), once on the second death. Double the on-death payload with a
  single card.

▸ Growth + Healer
  Healer restores HP to keep the Growing unit's gains from being chipped away.
  A Growth unit that also gets healed each turn maintains its investment and
  compounds faster.

▸ Smelt + Healer
  Smelt pays HP each turn for a repeating effect. Healer restores HP.
  If the Healer's output matches or exceeds the Smelt cost, the Smelt engine
  runs indefinitely at no net HP loss.

▸ Immunity + Taunt
  Immunity blocks spells, debuffs, and status effects. Taunt forces combat
  attacks into the unit. Together: spells can't remove it, status can't cripple
  it, and the opponent must commit real combat bodies to kill it. Extremely
  durable wall with no soft answer.

▸ Sleep + Sniper
  Sleep locks a target unit into place (heals but cannot act; wakes if hit).
  Sniper skips the sleeping front unit entirely and picks a back-row target.
  The sleeping blocker provides no protection while your Sniper freely eliminates
  whatever is behind it.

▸ Freeze (Tundra) + displacement (Reroute / Seaweed Octopus / Tidal Wave)
  Shove an enemy unit into the Tundra lane. The moment it enters, it Freezes
  (cannot act, blocks first hit but cannot retaliate). Your clock units freely
  attack without taking return damage while the frozen unit helplessly waits.
  Lane Control's primary tempo tool.

▸ Aquatic (triggered) + repeated Water lane entry
  Some Aquatic cards carry effect arrays that fire on entering the Water lane
  (not just the `true` flag). If a Mover or repeated relocation effect cycles
  a unit in and out of Water, the on-enter effects chain-fire. Niche but
  potentially infinite-value with the right setup.

▸ DoubleTeam + Overshot
  DoubleTeam allows two units to share a lane. Two Overshot units in one lane
  both push face damage every turn. Alternatively: Overshot in front + any
  aggressive body in back, both contributing value from a single contested lane.

▸ Bloodlust + Growth
  Independent snowball engines on one body. Growth adds stats at end of turn;
  Bloodlust adds stats on kills. They don't amplify each other directly, but
  a unit with both compounds from two separate sources and becomes an extreme
  long-game threat.

▸ Producer + high-cost payoffs (Ramp core)
  Producer generates element pips each turn, acting as a one-unit ramp engine.
  Compresses 5–6 cost finishers by 1–2 turns. The Ramp deck's entire identity
  depends on keeping Producers alive long enough to fire payoffs prematurely.

▸ Brittle + Sacrifice pump
  Brittle destroys itself after attacking once — but if that single attack is
  boosted very high via Sacrifice (destroying your own units for buffs), the
  one swing can be decisive. Brittle becomes a spike-damage delivery vehicle.


════════════════════════════════
C-TIER — Situational / minor upside
════════════════════════════════

▸ Taunt + Healer (self-target)
  Taunt absorbs all attacks; a self-Healer restores HP after each combat round.
  The wall becomes much harder to grind down, but neither keyword is dramatically
  better for having the other — just sustained durability.

▸ Pierce + high attack
  Pierce ignores all defensive keywords and hits the deepest unit in the lane.
  High attack ensures the pierce target (usually a low-HP support or Growth
  engine tucked in the back) dies outright rather than just taking a chip.

▸ Mover (self) + friendly lane hazards
  A self-Mover wanders randomly each turn. If you own the hazard environments
  (Cinder Field, Tundra) in specific lanes, position your Mover to wander AWAY
  from them. Against enemy Movers, friendly hazards across multiple lanes trap
  the wandering unit wherever it lands.

▸ Metamorphosis + stat buffs (Growth / Bloodlust)
  Metamorphosis transforms the unit every N turns. Buffs accumulated before
  transformation (from Growth or Bloodlust) carry into the transformed form,
  so the transformation fires from a stronger baseline. The window before
  transformation is therefore worth spending resources on the unit.

▸ DoubleTeam + Healer
  Two units in a lane; the Healer restores the more-damaged one each turn.
  Raw attrition advantage — opponents need to kill two bodies rather than one,
  and both stay healthier. Low ceiling but reliable incremental value.

▸ Burn / Poison (status) aimed at your own Polish unit
  If your opponent's Burn or Poison ticks hit a Polish unit you control, Polish
  triggers on each damage event and converts enemy status damage into permanent
  stat gains. Punishes status-heavy archetypes (DoT, Attrition) that try to
  grind your board.

▸ Strike Through + high attack
  Strike Through damages the blocker AND the leader simultaneously with the full
  attack value. High attack means both the blocker AND the leader take meaningful
  damage. Without high attack the leader ping is negligible; with it, Strike
  Through becomes a reliable face-clock on top of normal combat.


════════════════════════════════
THREE-WAY COMBOS (all three needed)
════════════════════════════════

▸ Overshot + Double Strike + Battle Ready  [S-tier ceiling]
  All three at once on a single body (via Combo foundation bond) is the
  highest-damage single-turn play in the game. The unit arrives, attacks twice,
  both hits bypass all blockers. Usually a game-ending swing.

▸ Lethal + Double Strike + Splash Damage  [A-tier]
  Double Strike attacks twice; each hit is Lethal; Splash covers three lanes.
  Theoretically kills up to six units in one combat (three lanes × two swings),
  clearing any board ever assembled.

▸ Spike + Taunt + Tough  [A-tier]
  Taunt pulls attacks in; Tough reduces damage taken; Spike fires at full value.
  Attackers lose HP to Spike, deal reduced damage, and cannot kill it quickly.
  Near-unkillable wall that punishes any attempt to engage.

▸ Growth + Immunity + Taunt  [A-tier]
  Immunity blocks removal; Taunt forces attacks in; Growth compounds each turn
  the opponent fails to kill it. The opponent is stuck attacking into a growing
  unit that cannot be removed by spells and must be killed by bodies — which is
  exactly the pace Growth wins at.

▸ Polish + Cinder Field + Bloodlust  [A-tier]
  Cinder Field ticks 1/turn → Polish fires → stats grow. If the Polish unit also
  has Bloodlust, each kill adds further stats on top. Two permanent-growth
  engines running simultaneously on one body, one from the environment and one
  from kills.
