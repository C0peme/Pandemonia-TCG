PANDEMONIA TCG — CARD & LEADER CREATION GUIDE
=============================================

All content lives in: src/cards/data/starter.ts
Cards are plain JavaScript objects validated by Zod schemas (src/cards/schema.ts).
All IDs must be unique kebab-case strings. Names are human-readable display strings.
Do not duplicate an existing ID.


===================
  THE FOUR ELEMENTS
===================

Each card belongs to one of four elements. Elements define playstyle identity and
determine which leaders can efficiently use element-cost cards.

  Fire    — Aggressive and direct. Fast, high-attack units; direct damage; burn synergies.
  Water   — Control and presence. Disruption, freeze, sleep, expel; aquatic synergies.
  Nature  — Resource management and growth. Ramp (Producers), Bloodlust, Growth, Metamorphosis.
  Earth   — Defensive and counter. Tough, Spike, Polish, Taunt; strong foundations.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ELEMENT TOOLKITS — what each element typically has access to
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  These are design conventions, not hard engine rules: the schema lets any card take
  any keyword. But keeping to an element's toolkit is what makes the elements feel
  distinct, and it is the basis for element-conditional content (e.g. Adventure relics
  that grant a keyword to units of one element — grant the element's SIGNATURE keyword).

  COMPLETE ABILITY -> ELEMENT MAP. Every keyword in the schema is assigned to exactly one
  element, 8 per element. This is the canonical list: it is what makes elements feel distinct,
  the basis for element-scoped content (relics, enhancements), and the reference for any future
  scheme that derives an ability's PIP from the ability's own element rather than the card's.

  It was previously incomplete and partly wrong: 10 of 32 keywords were unassigned, Earth held
  only 2 entries against Fire's and Nature's 7, and four abilities were filed under an element
  that no card actually used — Taunt and Spike were listed as Water/Nature while the cards put
  them overwhelmingly on Earth (10 and 7 carriers), and Overshot/Splash Damage were listed as
  Water while Fire carries most of them. Those four are reassigned to match the cards; Earth was
  not ability-poor, its two biggest mechanics were filed elsewhere.

  Fire   — aggression, burst & self-sacrifice. Signature: Battle Ready.
           Battle Ready, Strike Through, Double Strike, Brittle, Kamikaze, Smelt,
           Overshot, Splash Damage.  Status: Burn.
           Theme — hit first, hit hard, trade the body.

  Water  — control, positioning & denial. Signature: Aquatic.
           Aquatic, Sniper, Pierce, Shield, Double Team, Healer, Mover, Expel.
           Status: Freeze, Sleep.
           Theme — pick your targets, deny theirs, decide where everything stands.

  Nature — growth, resources & evolution. Signature: Growth and Bloodlust.
           Growth, Bloodlust, Producer, Airborne, Lethal, Metamorphosis, Sacrifice,
           Branch Shot.  Status: Poison.
           Theme — start small, snowball, out-resource.

  Earth  — defense, endurance & punishment. Signature: Tough.
           Tough, Polish, Taunt, Spike, True Shield, Immunity, Zombified, Debuff.
           Theme — absorb, punish attackers, win the long game.

  PIPS ARE DERIVED FROM THIS MAP. A card pays ONE PIP PER ABILITY, charged in the element of
  the ABILITY — not the element printed on the card. A Fire body carrying Taunt pays an Earth
  pip; Frost Imp is a Water card whose only ability is Overshot, so it costs 1 Fire. Cards
  become multi-element by what they DO. (`recommendedElements` in budget.ts; statuses carry
  their own element, so an on-hit Burn is Fire wherever it is printed.)

  Effects with NO canonical element — damage, draw, heal, summon, energy — earn NO PIP AT ALL.
  Their cost stays as plain energy. A pip is a statement that an ability belongs to an element;
  charging one for a colourless effect merely because of the card it happens to be printed on
  says nothing. So Firebolt (plain damage) is priced entirely in energy even though it is a Fire
  card, while Hypnotic Patterns (Sleep, a Water ability) converts its whole cost into a pip.

  `pierce` on a damage effect is the Pierce ABILITY wearing a flag instead of a keyword, so it
  colours that effect Water.

  NEUTRAL is the card class for cards with no elemental ability association at all: no pips, no
  element identity, priced purely in energy, castable on curve by every leader. It is a CLASS,
  not a fifth element — there is no neutral bank, no neutral cap and no neutral pip, which is
  why `Element` (costs, banking, leader caps) excludes it and `CardElement` (a card's own
  element) includes it.

  Being neutral is an AUTHORED choice, not a derived one. Plenty of elemental cards also have no
  element-bearing ability — a vanilla Fire 2/2 pays no pip either — but its element still says
  which leader wants it and which archetype it belongs to. Reserve Neutral for cards that belong
  to no element: mercenaries, scholars, salvage, a sharpened stake.

  This is a real constraint where energy is not. Energy equals the round number and is uncapped,
  so a bigger energy cost barely restrains a card after round ~5; pips are gated by a leader's
  elementCaps and by banking. It is a constraint, NOT a lock: `settleCost` pays any element
  shortfall out of generic energy, so an off-element ability makes a card EXPENSIVE for the
  wrong leader rather than uncastable.

  Totals are unchanged by the scheme — a card pays the same number of pips as before, just in
  different colours. Applying it across the pool moved 38 cards and left the pool's total pip
  count identical.

  These are design conventions, not engine rules: the schema lets any card take any keyword.
  Deliberately going off-element is how a card reads as a splash or a hybrid — but it should be
  a decision, not an accident.

  When adding element-scoped effects, prefer each element's signature keyword above:
  Fire→Battle Ready, Water→Aquatic, Nature→Growth/Bloodlust, Earth→Tough. NOTE: Aquatic
  is engine-special-cased and NOT in the grantable-keyword subset, so effects that GRANT
  a keyword (relic/enhancement keyword grants) use the element's next best instead —
  for Water that is Taunt (presence) or Sniper (control).


===================
  CARD TYPES
===================

All cards share these base fields:

  id          string (kebab-case, unique)
  name        string (display name)
  element     "fire" | "water" | "nature" | "earth"
  type        "unit" | "spell" | "foundation" | "environment"
  cost        { energy: number, elements?: [{ type: element, amount: 1–4 }, ...] }
  text        string (optional — human-readable rules text shown on card)
  tags        string[] (optional — e.g. ["signature"] hides the card in deck builder)
  wip         boolean (default false — marks unimplemented mechanics)


--- UNITS ---

Required extra fields:
  attack    number (≥ 0)
  hp        number (≥ 1)
  keywords  object (see KEYWORDS section; default {})

Optional:
  onHit     object (see ON-HIT section)
  onPlay    Effect[] (triggers when unit enters play)
  onAttack  Effect[] (triggers just before this unit attacks)
  endOfTurn Effect[] (triggers at end of this unit's owner's turn)
  startOfTurn Effect[] (triggers at start of this unit's owner's turn)


--- SPELLS ---

Required extra fields:
  effects   Effect[] (at least one; see EFFECTS section)


--- FOUNDATIONS ---

Required extra fields:
  attack    number (default 0)
  hp        number (≥ 1)
  keywords  object (foundation's OWN keywords while standalone)
  grants    {
    stat?:        { attack?: number, hp?: number }
    keywords?:    KeywordsObject
    onHit?:       OnHit
    onAttack?:    Effect[]
    endOfTurn?:   Effect[]
    startOfTurn?: Effect[]
  }

Foundations are placed standalone in a lane and bond with the next unit placed there.
The bonded unit receives grants for as long as they are stacked. Grants are full
value-transfer — the host runs them through normal engine machinery.

STAT CARRYOVER IS AUTOMATIC. Do NOT author `grants.stat` — every Foundation passes on HALF
its own printed stats, rounded down, and registry.ts derives that at build time. A 2/4
Foundation grants +1/+2; a 2/3 grants +1/+1. Authoring a stat grant is pointless: the rule
overrides it. Custom foundations from the Card Studio inherit it automatically.

COSTING: a Foundation is priced as its own body PLUS its granted KEYWORDS, both at full
value. Two things are deliberately NOT charged:
  - the half-stat carryover, because you already paid for those stats in the body, and it is
    a partial refund on a downside (bonding sacrifices the body; only half survives) rather
    than upside;
  - nothing else — the old 1.5 "delayed upside" discount on the whole grant is gone. A meta
    sim showed a deck built to bond reliably (8 foundations behind 22 units) realised the
    grant nearly every time, so that was a second discount stacked on the ability-pip one.

SURCHARGE: a Foundation then pays ONE EXTRA PIP on top. At exactly unit rates the plain unit
became pointless — same body, same keywords, same price, except the Foundation also hands half
its stats and all its keywords to a host. The extra pip is what it pays for being strictly more
flexible.

The surcharge is a genuine premium, not another energy->pip conversion: recommendedEnergy
subtracts only the ability-derived pips, so the pip is added with no energy coming off.

Net effect: a Foundation costs what a regular unit with the same body and keywords would,
PLUS one element pip. You pay unit rates and a premium, then trade the body away to move the
abilities onto a host.

Note: expansion-only keywords (producer, healer, debuff, mover, expel) do NOT work
as granted `keywords` entries. Grant their behaviour via trigger effects instead.
Example: a producer grant = grants: { endOfTurn: [{ kind: 'energyNext', amount: 1 }] }
Use `energyNext` for any END-OF-TURN energy (it lands on your next turn; plain `energy`
would be wiped by the turn reset). Plain `energy` is for immediate grants during your own
turn, and with an `element` it banks that element instead.

Battle Ready on bond: a unit placed on a foundation from a previous turn ignores
summoning sickness and may attack immediately. Same-turn drops do not.


--- ENVIRONMENTS ---

Required extra fields:
  lanes     string[] — which lane groups this env may be placed in.
              Use [] for any lane, or any of ['ground', 'heights', 'water'].
              'ground' matches both ground1 and ground2.
  effects   Effect[] (at least one; fires once on enter for units in the lane)

Optional:
  grantKeywords  KeywordsObject — keywords granted persistently to every unit in the lane.


===================
  EFFECTS
===================

Effects are objects with a `kind` and optional parameters.
They appear in: spell effects[], unit triggers, environment effects[], hero power effects[], kamikaze, polish effects[], bloodlust effects[].

  kind      (required)
  target    TargetScope (see TARGETING SCOPES)
  amount    number (damage, heal, buff stacks, etc.)
  status    status name (for applyStatus)
  stat      { attack?: number, hp?: number } (for buff/debuff)
  cardId    string (for conjure/summon)
  lane      LaneId (for summon — fixed destination)
  element   element name (for energy)
  note      string (for custom — human description only)

Kind reference:

  damage       Deal `amount` damage to target.
               chain: if the hit kills, splash `chain` damage to the weakest other enemy.
               chainDiminish: self-diminishing chain (amount, amount-1, amount-2… on each kill).
               amountFrom: 'targetAttack' — derive the hit from the TARGET instead of a
               printed `amount`: deals damage equal to the target's current attack. Priced at a
               proxy of 3 (you cast it on a real threat, not a random unit).

               DIFFERENTIATE REMOVAL BY CONDITION, NOT PRICE. Energy equals the round number
               and is uncapped, so after round ~5 a costlier answer is barely a worse answer —
               measured, +2 energy on 5 of a deck's 30 cards moved it 1.6pp, inside noise. A
               conditional answer is genuinely good against some boards and dead against others
               no matter how much energy the player has. Reprisal (Earth) kills a 5/5 and does
               nothing to a 0/5 wall; that is what balances it, not its cost.

               pierce: ignore the target's protective defences — Freeze, Shield, Tough, True
               Shield and Spike — exactly as the `pierce` KEYWORD does for a unit's attack.
               Only Immunity still stops it. Costs the same 1.0 the keyword table charges for
               Pierce.

               *** CARD DAMAGE IS A HIT. ***
               A damage effect resolves through the same path as a unit's attack (wakeOnHit in
               status.ts): it WAKES its target, and FREEZE ABSORBS IT — one hit, then the freeze
               is consumed. A spell is a hit from a card; it does not get to ignore defences
               that a swing respects.

               Two consequences when authoring:

               1. ORDER MATTERS. `[freeze, damage]` on one card is a bug: the freeze you just
                  applied eats your own damage. Write `[damage, freeze]` (see Frostbite
                  Harpoon). The engine will not warn you.
               2. A deck that freezes needs `pierce` to follow up, or it protects the very
                  units it means to answer. That is deliberate — it is the cost of freeze being
                  cheap, and the reason Pierce/pierce exist as the premium answer.
  heal         Restore `amount` HP to target (capped at maxHp).
  buff         Give target +attack/+hp and/or keyword grants (immunity, overshot, lethal, taunt,
               trueShield, airborne, battleReady, doubleStrike, bloodlust, pierce).
  debuff       Reduce target's attack/hp. Blocked by Immunity.
  applyStatus  Apply `status`: burn (amount=stacks), poison (amount=level), sleep, freeze,
               shield (amount=stacks), trueShield, zombified, taunt.
  draw         Draw `amount` cards.
  energy       Add `amount` energy NOW. With `element`, banks that element instead.
               `chooseElement: true` lets the player pick at cast time. Only valid during
               your own turn — see energyNext.
  energyNext   Queue `amount` energy onto the recipient's NEXT turn (consumed there).
               Use this for anything that fires at END of turn: beginTurn overwrites
               `energy` with the round number, so plain `energy` would be discarded.
  bankMax      Fill every one of the target's element banks to its cap. Takes no `amount`.
               How much it is worth depends on the leader's elementCaps and what is already
               banked, so it is strongest for a leader with wide caps and an empty bank.
  move         Move target unit to another lane.
  expel        Return target unit to its owner's hand.
  forget       Remove `amount` cards from the top of a player's deck.
               `target: 'enemy'` mills opponent; omit for self.
  conjure      Create card `cardId` in a hand. `target: 'enemy'` = opponent's hand.
  summon       Summon unit `cardId` onto the board. `lane` fixes destination.
  extraAction  Grant target unit a bonus attack this turn (no retaliation).
  costMod      Adjust costs. `cardType` selects type ('unit'|'spell'|'foundation'|'environment'|'all').
               Positive raises cost, negative lowers it. `target: 'enemy'` hits the opponent.
               -99 effectively makes a type free (engine clamps at 0). Lasts until end of turn.
  setStats     Overwrite target's base attack / max HP to fixed values. Blocked by Immunity.
  cleanse      Remove all status effects from target.
  custom       Placeholder for unimplemented mechanics. Always include `note`.


===================
  TARGETING SCOPES
===================

  self         The triggering unit itself.
  leader       Own leader (beneficial) or enemy leader (harmful). Always fires.
  killer       The unit that dealt the killing blow (Kamikaze effects only).
  any          Interactive: player picks any unit or leader on either side.
  ally         Single ally: most-hurt same-lane first, then board-wide.
  enemy        Single enemy: same-lane first, then weakest anywhere.
  all-ally     AOE: every allied unit (excluding self).
  all-enemy    AOE: every enemy unit.
  lane-ally    Single ally in the same lane only.
  lane-enemy   Single enemy in the same lane only.
  leaderUnit   The caster's own leader-unit. Only valid for leaders with leaderUnitCardId.


===================
  KEYWORDS
===================

Keywords are fields on the `keywords` object. Omit (do not set false) to leave inactive.

--- Attack / Targeting ---

  overshot: true          Strikes the opposing leader over units (Airborne intercepts).
  pierce: true         Strikes deepest target, piercing Shield/Tough (not Immunity).
  sniper: true            From Heights or Airborne: choose which enemy lane to strike.
  branchShot: true        Attack also hits an adjacent ground lane simultaneously.
  splashDamage: true      Damage to front unit also hits the back unit in the same lane.
  doubleStrike: true      Attacks twice per combat step; both hits trigger on-hit effects.
  airborne: true          Fights in Heights. Can attack into any non-Heights lane.
                          Cancels Aquatic's Water bonus.
  battleReady: true       Ignores summoning sickness; may act the turn it enters play.

--- Defensive ---

  shield: N               Blocks N incoming damage instances entirely.
  trueShield: true        Blocks ALL incoming damage until start of owner's next turn.
  taunt: true             Enemies must target this unit before any other in its lane.
  spike: N                Deals N damage back to any unit that attacks it.
  immunity: true          Blocks harmful control effects: statuses, debuff, setStats,
                          displacement (move/expel), and Lethal/Pierce. Does NOT block
                          damage or beneficial effects.
  tough: N                Reduces all incoming damage by N (minimum 1 gets through).
  doubleTeam: true        Allows a second unit to occupy this lane alongside this unit.
  polish: { stat?, effects? }
                          Fires every time this unit takes damage. Applies stat gain and/or effects.
                          Example: { stat: { attack: 1, hp: 0 } }

--- Support / Triggered ---

  healer: { amount, target, trigger }
                          Heals `amount` to `target` when `trigger` fires.
                          Trigger: 'onPlay' | 'onAttack' | 'endOfTurn' | 'startOfTurn'
  growth: { attack?, hp? }
                          Gains these stats at end of each owner's turn.
  producer: { amount }
                          Queues `amount` GENERIC energy onto the owner's NEXT turn. Generic,
                          not element-specific: pips are value-gated (see PIP_STEP), so most
                          cards cost no pips and elemental output had little left to buy.
                          Generic energy is always spendable and ignores the element caps.
                          It expands to an `energyNext` effect, NOT `energy`: beginTurn
                          overwrites `energy` with the round number, so end-of-turn energy
                          added to the current pool would be silently discarded.
  metamorphosis: { everyTurns, into?, gains? }
                          After `everyTurns` turns, transforms into card `into` or gains `gains`.
  bloodlust: { buff?, effects? }
                          Fires on each kill. Gains `buff` and/or runs `effects`.
  kamikaze: Effect        Single effect that fires when this unit dies.
  sacrifice: { max, buff }
                          On play, may sacrifice up to `max` allies. Each sacrifice applies `buff`.
  mover: { scope, trigger? }
                          Moves a unit of `scope` ('ally'|'enemy'|'either'|'self').
                          Without trigger: fires on play. With 'endOfTurn'/'startOfTurn': fires each turn.
  expel: { scope }        Returns a unit to its owner's hand on play.
  debuff: { attack?, hp?, target? }
                          Stat reduction applied to target on play.
  brittle: true           Destroys itself after attacking once.
  zombified: true         On reaching 0 HP, revives at 1 HP once (then removed).
  aquatic: true | StatMod true = immune to Drowning. StatMod = also gains those stats in Water.
  smelt: { hpCost, effect }
                          Pay `hpCost` HP to activate `effect`. Cannot be self-lethal.


--- ON-HIT ---

onHit fires when this unit's attack deals damage to another unit.

  onHit: {
    burn?: number        — inflict Burn N on the target
    poison?: true | N    — inflict Poison (true = level 1; N = custom level)
    sleep?: number       — put the target to Sleep (N = heal-per-sleeping-turn / Wakeup-Shock bonus)
    freeze?: true        — inflict Freeze on the target
    zombified?: true     — inflict Zombified on the target
  }


===================
  LEADERS
===================

Leaders are objects in the rawLeaders array.

  id              string (kebab-case, unique)
  name            string
  element         "fire" | "water" | "nature" | "earth"
  elementCaps     { fire: N, water: N, nature: N, earth: N }  — must sum to exactly 8
  heroPower       { name, cost, hpCost?, effects, text? }  — usable once per turn
  signatureCardId string — card delivered to hand when leader HP drops to HALF its max
                           (15 for the standard 30-HP leader; scales for other max HP)

Element cap guidelines:
  Primary element: 3–4. Secondary: 2. Remaining: 1 each.
  Reserve cap 4 for archetypes with an expensive endgame payoff (Control, Stall, Ramp, Snowball, Combo).
  Fast archetypes (Aggro, Swarm) should use even or low spreads (e.g. 2/2/2/2) — they never
  bank 3–4 of one element, so a high cap wastes the slot.

The Signature card should:
  - Cost 0 energy
  - Be powerful enough to turn a close game but not instantly win
  - Match the leader's element and playstyle
  - Have a unique id prefixed with 'sig-'


===================
  DECKS
===================

  parseDeck({ name, leaderId, cards: [{ cardId, count }, ...] })

Rules:
  - Exactly 30 cards total.
  - Max 4 copies of any single card.
  - leaderId must match an existing leader.
  - All cardIds must match existing cards.

Archetype descriptions:
  Aggro        — cheap high-attack units; direct damage; win fast or lose
  DoT          — spread Burn and Poison; win by chip damage over time
  Control      — disable with Sleep/Freeze; deny the board; win late
  Midrange     — flexible, no hard specialisation; wins on card quality
  Stall        — wall every lane with Shield/Taunt/Tough; outlast
  Guardian     — protect a leader-unit (Riku) and grow it into the win condition
  Attrition    — Spike, Poison, Polish; punish every trade
  Snowball     — grow units enormous via Growth/Bloodlust/Foundations
  Ramp         — Producer units to play expensive cards early
  Combo        — bond grant-foundations onto a body for a same-turn swing
  Deck Out     — mill the opponent's deck; Null cards bleed their leader
  Swarm        — go wide with many small bodies behind an anthem
  Lane Control — Movers, Environments, hazard zones to control positioning


===================
  BALANCING GUIDELINES
===================

The budget calculator lives in src/cards/budget.ts. Run `npm test` and check
src/cards/cost_diff.test.ts to see how your card's value compares to its energy cost.

--- HOW BUDGET WORKS ---

A card's energy cost converts to a budget:
  budget = energy + 0.5 × (total element pip count)

Example: cost { energy: 3, elements: [{ type: 'fire', amount: 1 }] } → budget = 3.5

The card's computed value must roughly match its budget. A value ≈ budget ± 0.5 is fine.
Slightly under is acceptable (a small efficiency incentive); significantly over is too strong.

--- TWO KEY MULTIPLIERS ---

All abilities (keywords, effects, on-hit) are discounted 20% versus raw stats:
  effective ability cost = base cost × 0.8

Recurring effects (endOfTurn / startOfTurn / onAttack) get an additional ×1.4:
  effective recurring cost = base cost × 0.8 × 1.4

AOE effects (all-ally / all-enemy scopes, and environment grantKeywords) get ×2.5:
  effective AOE cost = base cost × 2.5 × 0.8

--- STAT COSTS (GEOMETRIC) ---

Unit base stats use a geometric curve — each extra point costs progressively more.
Use the budget calculator (cost_diff test) to verify; the approximate values are:

  attack 1 = 0.38 | 2 = 0.87 | 3 = 1.51 | 4 = 2.31 | 5 = 3.32 | 6 = 4.57
  hp     1 = 0.38 | 2 = 0.91 | 3 = 1.65 | 4 = 2.67 | 5 = 4.07 | 6 = 5.99

These are the unit body costs BEFORE any keyword or effect costs.
For foundation grants and buff/debuff effects, stats use the flat rate: +0.2 per attack, +0.4 per hp (flat × 0.8).

--- KEYWORD COSTS (effective, after ×0.8) ---

Drawbacks:
  Brittle                          −0.40
  Kamikaze (+ effect cost)         −0.40  — effect's effective cost is added separately
  Sacrifice (+ buff stat cost)     −0.40  — buff stats use the flat rate

Positional / tactical:
  Battle Ready                     +0.20
  Sniper                           +0.60
  Overshot                         +0.60
  Airborne                         +0.60
  Aquatic (true)                   +0.20  — bonus stats add flat cost
  Taunt                            +0.80
  Double Team                      +0.80
  Branch Shot                      +1.20
  Splash Damage                    +1.20
  Strike Through                   +1.20
  Double Strike                    +1.20

Defensive:
  Spike 1 / +0.40 each stage       +1.20
  Tough 1 / +1.20 each stage       +1.60
  Shield 1 / +0.40 each stage      +2.00
  True Shield                      +2.40
  Immunity                         +1.60
  Pierce                        +1.60
  Lethal                           +2.40

Triggered:
  Growth (+ flat buff cost)        +1.20
  Bloodlust (+ flat buff cost)     +1.00
  Polish (+ flat buff/effect cost) +0.80
  Producer N (per unit output)     +0.56 each  — e.g. Producer 2 = +1.12
  Healer N (per HP healed)         +0.56 each
  Mover                            +1.12  — recurring move effect
  Expel (keyword, on-play)         +2.80
  Debuff (+ flat stat cost)        +1.40  — recurring debuff premium baked in
  Zombified                        +2.20

--- EFFECT COSTS (effective, after ×0.8) ---

These are costs when effects appear in spells, onPlay, or triggered arrays:

  damage N (single)                N × 0.40
  damage N (all-enemy / all-ally)  N × 1.00
  damage, chain / chainDiminish    add +0.80 on top
  heal N (single)                  N × 0.40
  buff stat (flat rates)           +0.20/attack, +0.40/hp + any keyword costs
  debuff (single)                  +1.00 + flat stat cost
  debuff (AOE)                     +2.50 + flat stat cost × 2.5
  applyStatus burn 1 / +0.80 each  +0.80
  applyStatus poison               +0.80
  applyStatus sleep                +1.60
  applyStatus freeze               +2.00
  applyStatus shield 1 / +0.40 ea  +2.00
  applyStatus trueShield           +2.40
  applyStatus zombified            +2.20
  applyStatus taunt                +0.80
  draw 1 / +0.80 each              +0.80
  energy N                         N × 0.40
  move (single)                    +0.80
  expel (single)                   +2.00
  forget 1 / +1.20 each extra      +1.40
  conjure                          +1.20
  summon (value of the card)       full card value
  extraAction                      +1.20
  costMod                          +0.80
  setStats                         +1.60
  cleanse                          +0.80
  custom                           +0.00

Recurring trigger: multiply by 1.4. Example: endOfTurn heal 1 = 0.40 × 1.4 = 0.56.

--- ON-HIT COSTS (effective) ---

On-hit fires every time the unit's attack damages a target, so it has a ×1.5 recurrence
premium on top of the ×0.8 ability factor (base × 1.5 × 0.8):

  on-hit burn 1     +1.20
  on-hit burn 2     +2.40
  on-hit poison     +1.20
  on-hit sleep      +2.40
  on-hit freeze     +3.00

--- ELEMENT COST ACCESSIBILITY ---

  1 pip of any element  — accessible to all leaders
  2 same-element pips   — accessible to most; some struggle
  3 same-element pips   — restricted to leaders with a high cap; contains the strongest cards
  4 same-element pips   — core payoff for a specific archetype

--- HOW MANY PIPS? (ONE PER ABILITY) ---

Do NOT hand-pick a cost. Both halves come from src/cards/budget.ts:

  pips   = abilityCount, capped at 4 and at the card own rounded value
  energy = round(value) - pips

Each ability converts ONE point of cost out of generic energy and into an element pip.
Nominally that is the same total cost, but it is cheaper in practice: a pip is paid from
end-of-turn overflow that would otherwise evaporate, while generic energy competes with
everything else you want to do this turn.

That conversion is the whole reason ability cards can compete with vanilla stat-sticks.
It replaced an older fudge where printed stats were taxed (STAT_BASE 0.38) and abilities
discounted (ABILITY_FACTOR 0.8) so the two errors would cancel. Both are now honest
(0.28 and 1.0) and the advantage is structural instead.

It also gives elements a job. A vanilla body is pure energy and always castable; an ability
card commits you to an element, so a leader elementCaps decide which ability cards their
deck can support, and banking has a reason to exist.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN ABILITY: anything that ADDS value - each value-positive keyword, each
on-hit status, each trigger effect, each environment effect, and a foundation grants.
A pure downside does NOT count (Brittle earns no pip). Stats are not abilities. Aquatic:true
is a lane permission, not an ability; aquatic with effects is.

EVERY type is included, spells among them. There is NO floor on the generic half either: an
ability-dense card can convert its cost away entirely and print as 0 energy + N pips. That is
safe because pips FALL BACK to generic energy - a 0-energy/2-pip card still costs 2 to a
player who never banked the element, so it is free only to one who committed to it. Note that
a 0-energy card cannot receive an Adventure cost-reduction enhancement (nothing left to cut).

PAYING AN ELEMENT COST: pips draw from that element bank FIRST, and any shortfall is charged
to generic energy at 1:1. So a pip is a DISCOUNT, never a gate - an ability card is cheaper
when you have committed to its element and costs full face value when you have not. No card
in hand is ever uncastable for want of the right bank.

VALUE_SCALE (1.20) pins the pool absolute price level so removing the old fudges did not
make every card cheaper. It is not a balance knob - scaling every card together changes no
card cost relative to any other. Retune it only to move the game overall pace.

--- WORKED EXAMPLE ---

Magma Brute: cost { energy: 2, elements: [{ type: 'fire', amount: 1 }] }
  → budget = 2.5
  Stats: attack 3 → 1.51, hp 2 → 0.91 → body = 2.42
  Brittle: −0.40
  Total value = 2.02  ✓ (slightly under 2.5 — acceptable)

Ember Tick: cost { energy: 2, elements: [{ type: 'fire', amount: 1 }] }
  → budget = 2.5
  Stats: attack 1 → 0.38, hp 2 → 0.91 → body = 1.29
  on-hit burn 1 = 1.20
  Total value = 2.49  ✓


===================
  ADDING CONTENT TO THE CODEBASE
===================

1. Add card objects to rawCards in src/cards/data/starter.ts (keep entries on one line).
2. Add leader objects to rawLeaders in src/cards/data/starter.ts.
3. Add deck objects at the bottom of starter.ts using parseDeck, exported as a named const.
4. Run `npm test` to validate schemas and check cost_diff for budget alignment.
5. All new card/leader IDs must be unique across the entire file.
